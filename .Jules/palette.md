## 2024-07-25 - Descriptive Alt Text for Images
**Learning:** Images without descriptive `alt` text are inaccessible to screen reader users. Generic or placeholder text like "image" or "streak" provides no context. This is a critical accessibility issue that can prevent users from understanding the content and purpose of the images on a page.
**Action:** During every review, I will specifically check for images and ensure they have meaningful `alt` text that accurately describes the image's content and function. For purely decorative images, I will use an empty `alt=""` attribute.
